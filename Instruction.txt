STEP 1: KILL THE VIRUS FIRST
-------------------------------------------------------------------
Run a deep scan with Malwarebytes or Dr.Web CureIt! and delete all 
threats. If you do not kill the virus first, it will re-infect your 
files immediately.

STEP 2: RUN THE CLEANER
-------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Download "cleaner.ps1".
2. Right-click on the file and select "Run with PowerShell".
3. Click "Yes" when Windows asks for Admin rights.

STEP 3: RESTORE YOUR FILES
-------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Type "1" for Safe Clean (recommended) or "3" if your files are 
   just faded/semi-transparent.
2. Paste the full path to your infected folder or drive 
   (e.g., D:\ or C:\Users\Name\Downloads).
3. Press Enter and wait for the completion message.
